- a working mobile-first MVP,

- a complementary eligible team,

## 2. Locked product identity

**Product:** RIPPLE BD  
**Core experience:** CHAOS → PEACE  
**Tagline:** **Every click has a consequence.**  
**Protection module:** SHIELD Mode  
**Campaign:** **#EveryClickRipples**  
**Primary DKC theme:** Responsible Digital Citizenship  
**Cross-cutting lens:** Digital Literacy

### One-sentence product definition

> RIPPLE BD is a mobile-first digital peace simulator where Bangladeshi youth experience how online harm spreads, rewind the consequences, practise safer responses, and measure whether those digital reflexes last.

### Core loop

**MIRROR → RIPPLE → HUMAN CONSEQUENCE → REWIND → PEACE → REFLECT → RETAIN**

---

## 3. What the MVP must demonstrate

The MVP is a **6–8 minute polished vertical slice**, not a giant platform.

It must include:

1. **Nodi**, a fictional digital community.
2. **MIRROR** — user makes a natural first decision without being taught first.
3. **Ripple Engine** — the decision creates visible social consequences.
4. **Human consequence** — harm reaches a fictional person/community.
5. **REWIND** — the timeline reverses.
6. **PEACE retry** — user practises a safer response.
7. **Manipulation X-Ray** — user sees how harmful content manipulates attention/judgment.
8. **SHIELD Mode** — Recognize → Support → Preserve → Report → Escalate.
9. **Digital Reflex Profile** — scenario-based learning feedback.
10. **Booster-ready flow** — Day 3 and Day 7 retention testing.

## 4. Locked MVP scenarios

### Scenario 1 — The Cropped Clip
Focus: misinformation, source verification, de-escalation.  
Context: interfaith tension.

### Scenario 2 — The Viral Meme
Focus: cyberbullying, gendered harassment, bystander action.  
Includes: SHIELD Mode.

### Scenario 3 — The Missing Voice
Focus: source diversity, representation, authentic voice.  
Context: ethnic/minority inclusion.

All scenarios are **fictional composites**. No real incident, real victim, real political actor, or fabricated allegation against a real community.

## 5. Evidence model

RIPPLE is inspired by proven mechanisms, not copied from one project.

Global evidence base includes:
- **Bad News** — active inoculation / learning manipulation techniques.
- **Harmony Square** — randomized evidence that a simulated misinformation game can improve recognition and reduce willingness to share manipulative content.
- **Jigsaw/Cambridge prebunking work** — scalable, short-form inoculation interventions.
- **Right To Be / HeartMob** — practical bystander-support models.
- **Bangladesh/UN Women interactive storytelling work** — local precedent for interactive digital engagement.

Our own claims must come only from our own data.

### Never do
- invent pilot numbers,
- invent testimonials,
- claim causation from a tiny uncontrolled pilot,
- claim “world first” without proof,
- claim a partnership that does not exist.

## 8. Primary impact measurement

### Digital Peace Decision Score (DPDS)

100-point composite:
- Verification / responsible sharing — 25
- Manipulation recognition — 25
- Safe bystander response — 25
- Support + de-escalation — 25

### Proposed fellowship target

By February 2027:
- at least 200 youth complete RIPPLE BD,
- average DPDS improves by at least 20 percentage points from baseline,
- follow-up participants retain at least a 10-point improvement at Day 7.

These are targets, not current results.

### Pre-submission proof target

Pilot:
- 20–30 youth,
- baseline,
- MVP,
- immediate post,
- optional Day-7 follow-up,
- completion rate,
- usability feedback,
- one honest chart,
- one product improvement based on pilot feedback.

## 9. What judges should remember

The signature demo is:

1. judge sees a viral fictional post,
2. judge chooses SHARE or VERIFY,
3. Ripple Engine shows the consequence,
4. a person/community is affected,
5. screen says **REWIND**,
6. judge retries with safer tools,
7. presenter says:

> “That was one click. RIPPLE BD is built around a simple idea: young people learn digital peacebuilding more deeply when they experience the consequence of a decision, reverse it, and practise a safer response.”

The memorable product identity is **not a quiz**. It is **consequence → rewind → practice → retention**.
