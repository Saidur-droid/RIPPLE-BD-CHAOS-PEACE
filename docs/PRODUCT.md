# Product Strategy

## 1. Positioning

RIPPLE BD is **not** a generic awareness campaign, a fact-checking quiz, a chatbot, or a victim-reporting service.

It is a **Digital Peace Simulator**: a safe training ground where young people make realistic online decisions, watch the social consequences unfold, rewind the harm, and practise better responses.

### One-line pitch

> RIPPLE BD lets young people experience how online harm spreads, rewind the consequences, practise safer responses, and measure whether those digital reflexes last.

## 2. Primary audience

Initial MVP:
- Bangladeshi youth aged roughly 18–30,
- university/college/youth-community settings,
- mobile-first,
- Bangla/Banglish-friendly interaction,
- first pilot in the team's own division.

## 3. DKC positioning

**Main theme:** Responsible Digital Citizenship  
**Cross-cutting lens:** Digital Literacy

Other DKC themes appear as scenario contexts:
- Cyberbullying & Online Harassment
- Interfaith Harmony & Religious Tolerance
- Inclusion of Ethnic Minority & Indigenous Voices
- Gender-based Online Violence

We do **not** claim to solve all five themes. We claim to strengthen responsible digital decision-making using scenarios that reflect multiple forms of online harm.

## 4. Core loop

**MIRROR → RIPPLE → HUMAN CONSEQUENCE → REWIND → PEACE → REFLECT → RETAIN**

### MIRROR
Capture the user's natural first response without teaching first.

### RIPPLE
Show immediate, secondary, human, and community consequences.

### REWIND
Reverse the timeline visually and return to the decision point.

### PEACE
Unlock safer response tools and let the user retry.

### REFLECT
Explain the mechanism, not just the “correct answer”.

### RETAIN
Re-test via short booster scenarios after the main experience.

## 5. Signature product moments

### A. Ripple Engine
A single click creates a visible causal chain:
Action → reach → reaction → targeting → trust/safety shift.

### B. REWIND
The interface reverses the damage and returns the user to the original decision.

### C. Manipulation X-Ray
The user dissects a post into mechanisms such as:
- emotional urgency,
- missing source,
- social proof,
- us-vs-them framing,
- cropped context,
- impersonation.

### D. SHIELD Mode
When a person becomes the target of harm, the user practises:
**Recognize → Support → Preserve → Report → Escalate**

This is a training protocol, not a real-time emergency or reporting service.

### E. Digital Reflex Profile
The user receives a scenario-based learning profile across:
- verification,
- manipulation recognition,
- bystander response,
- support,
- de-escalation.

## 6. What makes RIPPLE different

RIPPLE does not stop at “spot the fake”.
It connects:
- individual decisions,
- social amplification,
- human harm,
- community trust,
- bystander action,
- repair,
- retention.

## 7. MVP success condition

A first-time user should, within 60 seconds, understand:
1. what decision they made,
2. what ripple it caused,
3. why it mattered,
4. how they can respond differently.

The full MVP should feel complete in **6–8 minutes**.

## 8. North-star statement

> Prevent harm when possible. Interrupt it when it begins. Protect people when harm happens.
