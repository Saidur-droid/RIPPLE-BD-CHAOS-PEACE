# Pitch Strategy

## Principle

Do not spend the first minute explaining the product.

Let the judge **experience one decision**.

## Signature opening

Show a fictional viral post.

> “A video is exploding online. 8.4K shares in 20 minutes. What do you do?”

Buttons:
**SHARE** / **VERIFY**

Judge chooses.

The screen shows a short simulated ripple.

Then:
> “That was one click. RIPPLE BD is built around a simple idea: young people learn digital peacebuilding more deeply when they experience the consequence of a decision, reverse it, and practise a safer response.”

## 8-slide architecture

### Slide 1 — The Hook
Every click has a consequence.
One local/credible problem signal, not a wall of statistics.

### Slide 2 — The Gap
Online harms are taught separately, but youth experience them in the same feed.
Passive awareness ≠ decision practice.

### Slide 3 — RIPPLE BD
MIRROR → RIPPLE → REWIND → PEACE → RETAIN.

### Slide 4 — Live Product
Three signature moments:
- consequence chain,
- REWIND,
- SHIELD/X-Ray.

QR to prototype.

### Slide 5 — Evidence
Global research basis + local pilot.
Clearly separate external evidence from our pilot.

### Slide 6 — Implementation
Ripple Labs + digital simulator + boosters + #EveryClickRipples.

### Slide 7 — Measurement + Feasibility
DPDS, baseline/post/Day-7, zero-paid MVP stack, realistic budget.

### Slide 8 — Why Us / Ask
Three complementary team roles.
Community access.
What DKC funding/mentorship unlocks by February.

## Never say

- “world first” unless rigorously proven;
- “AI-powered” if it is not;
- “we will eliminate misinformation”;
- “our pilot proves causation”;
- “we partner with X” without confirmation;
- “we solve all five DKC themes.”

## Strong language

Use:
- prototype,
- test,
- measure,
- improve,
- retain,
- co-design,
- local pilot,
- scenario-based decision-making,
- digital peacebuilding.

## Judge questions to prepare

1. Why a game instead of awareness videos?
2. What is original vs inspired by prior work?
3. How do you know learning lasts?
4. How will you avoid stereotypes?
5. Why is this feasible with BDT 50,000?
6. How will you recruit participants?
7. What if the pilot result is weak?
8. How is SHIELD Mode safe?
9. Why is your team uniquely able to execute?
10. What will exist by February that does not exist today?

## Best answer to “What is innovative?”

> We are not claiming that simulation or prebunking is new. Our innovation is the Bangladesh-context closed learning loop: natural decision → visible social ripple → human consequence → rewind → safer retry → bystander protection → retention measurement. The product connects prevention, response, and measurement in one short experience.
