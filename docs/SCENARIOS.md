# Scenario Bible

All scenarios are fictional composites. They must not resemble an identifiable real person, active communal incident, political campaign, or ongoing criminal allegation.

## Scenario 1 — The Cropped Clip

### Purpose
Teach verification, context checking, and de-escalation.

### Setup
A short video clip appears in Nodi with an urgent caption claiming that members of a religious community committed a shocking act. The clip is cropped and lacks a source.

### MIRROR choices
- Share now
- Angry comment
- Verify source
- Ignore

### Harm chain
Share → reach increases → hostile comments increase → identity-based generalization appears → one fictional student is targeted → community trust falls.

### Manipulation X-Ray
- emotional urgency,
- missing original source,
- cropped context,
- social proof,
- us-vs-them framing.

### PEACE path
- search for original context inside the simulation,
- avoid group generalization,
- add corrective context,
- de-escalate comments,
- do not amplify unverified material.

### DKC relevance
Responsible Digital Citizenship + Interfaith Harmony context.

## Scenario 2 — The Viral Meme

### Purpose
Teach bystander action and gender-aware online safety.

### Setup
A meme mocks a fictional female student's appearance and spreads in a campus-like feed. The “joke” becomes a pile-on and then turns into sexualized comments and hostile DMs.

### MIRROR choices
- React/laugh
- Re-share
- Ignore
- Check on target
- Report

### Harm chain
Meme → pile-on → sexist comments → harassment → withdrawal/fear.

### SHIELD Mode
**Recognize**
Name the behavior without blaming the target.

**Support**
Privately check what the targeted person wants.

**Preserve**
Save necessary evidence without recirculating harmful content.

**Report**
Use the appropriate platform/community pathway.

**Escalate**
If credible threats or severe abuse are present, move to appropriate trusted/official support.

### Safety constraint
No sexual images, explicit abuse, or real victim content is used.

### DKC relevance
Cyberbullying + Gender-based Online Violence context.

## Scenario 3 — The Missing Voice

### Purpose
Teach source diversity, representation, and authentic voice.

### Setup
A post makes a sweeping claim about a fictional minority community. Everyone is commenting about the group, but no first-person source from that community is present.

### MIRROR choices
- share outsider interpretation,
- add stereotype-based comment,
- seek primary source,
- look for first-person context,
- pause.

### Harm chain
Unverified claim → stereotype reinforcement → exclusion → hostile framing → community voice disappears further.

### PEACE path
- identify who is missing,
- distinguish claim from evidence,
- seek a relevant first-person or primary source,
- avoid tokenization,
- add context without speaking over the community.

### DKC relevance
Responsible Digital Citizenship + Inclusion of Ethnic Minority and Indigenous Voices context.

# Writing rules

1. Never use names of real current politicians or parties.
2. Never portray a real faith or ethnic group as responsible for a fabricated incident.
3. Avoid trauma porn.
4. Avoid “perfect victim” assumptions.
5. Do not reward public confrontation as the only brave response.
6. Safer choices may have trade-offs; explain them.
7. “Correct” does not mean “morally superior person”; it means a safer evidence-informed decision in the scenario.
8. Bangla/Banglish copy should sound like actual youth communication, not textbook language.
