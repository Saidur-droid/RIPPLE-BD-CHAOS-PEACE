# Final initiative description (under 500 words)

**RIPPLE BD — CHAOS -> PEACE** is a mobile-first digital peacebuilding experience for young people in **[TARGET DIVISION]**. Its primary theme is **Responsible Digital Citizenship**, with **Digital Literacy** as the cross-cutting lens. The problem we address is simple: young people are regularly exposed to misinformation, cyberbullying, gendered abuse, exclusion and other online harms, but most awareness efforts explain these risks passively. In a real feed, decisions are made quickly. Knowing the right answer is different from practising the right response under pressure.

RIPPLE BD turns that gap into an interactive learning loop. A participant first encounters a fictional but locally understandable online scenario and makes a natural decision through **MIRROR**. The **Ripple Engine** then shows how that click can affect other people and communities. The participant can **REWIND** the moment, try a safer response through **PEACE**, inspect manipulation techniques through **Manipulation X-Ray**, and learn practical bystander-protection steps through **SHIELD Mode**. The experience ends with a **Digital Reflex Profile** that reflects scenario-based decision quality rather than judging identity or beliefs. All scenarios are fictional composites; the platform does not collect victim stories or sensitive political, religious or ethnic data.

A working Bangla-and-English mobile prototype already exists, with a reusable scenario system and zero-paid-software dependency. During the fellowship, our team will run facilitated **Ripple Labs** in [TARGET DIVISION], recruit youth through local educational and community networks, improve the scenarios through participant feedback, and combine the simulator with short follow-up boosters. We will use the BDT 50,000 seed grant for community sessions, participant access, content production/localization, implementation materials, measurement and documentation.

Our measurable outcome by **February 2027** is for at least **200 young people** in the target division to complete RIPPLE BD, with the average scenario-based **Digital Peace Decision Score (DPDS)** improving by at least **20 percentage points** from baseline immediately after the experience, and follow-up participants retaining at least a **10-point improvement after seven days**. We will report sample size, completion, missing follow-up and limitations honestly.

RIPPLE BD is not designed to tell young people what to think. It is designed to help them practise how to pause, verify, protect others and respond more safely when one click can create a much larger ripple.
