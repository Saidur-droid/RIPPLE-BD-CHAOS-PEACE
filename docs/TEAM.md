# Team Plan

## Eligibility first

DKC requires a **3–5 member team from the same division**. Therefore the minimum viable team is **3 people**, not 2.

The preferred structure is a **3-woman complementary team**, provided every member meets the official eligibility rules.

Official source:
https://www.digitalkhichurichallenge.com/en/apply

## Team shape

### 1. Product & Technology Lead
Owns:
- frontend,
- interaction design,
- scenario engine,
- deployment,
- live demo reliability.

Ideal evidence:
- web/app/Figma work,
- fast prototyping,
- product judgment.

### 2. Research, M&E & Safeguarding Lead
Owns:
- evidence base,
- scenario review,
- pilot design,
- DPDS,
- ethics/safeguarding,
- data quality.

Ideal evidence:
- research/social science/public health/NGO/M&E work.

### 3. Community, Creative & Pitch Lead
Owns:
- participant recruitment,
- Ripple Lab facilitation,
- Bangla/Banglish copy,
- campaign content,
- partnerships,
- pitch delivery.

Ideal evidence:
- campus/youth/community access,
- content/storytelling,
- facilitation/public speaking.

## Recruitment rule

No “name-only” member.

Each teammate must strengthen at least one DKC judging criterion.

## 30-minute audition tasks

**Tech candidate**
Build one clickable social-feed decision screen.

**Research candidate**
Write one harm scenario and define what behavior to measure.

**Community/creative candidate**
Explain how she would recruit 10 pilot participants and record a 30-second project pitch.

## Availability filter

Before selection ask:
- Can you commit meaningful time every day until submission?
- Can you attend required fellowship activities if selected?
- Are you comfortable owning a measurable deliverable?

## Recruitment copy

> **Looking for women teammates — RIPPLE BD / DKC 2026**
>
> We are building RIPPLE BD, an interactive digital peace simulator that lets young people experience how misinformation, cyberbullying, gendered abuse and exclusion spread online — then rewind the harm and practise safer responses.
>
> We are looking for strong contributors in product/tech, research & safeguarding, or community/content/storytelling. This is not a name-only team: we are building and piloting before the application deadline.
>
> Send: strongest skill, one relevant project/work example, current availability, and why digital safety/social cohesion matters to you.

## Interview principle

Select for:
- execution,
- reliability,
- complementary skill,
- community access,
- ethical judgment,
- communication.

Not for CV prestige alone.
