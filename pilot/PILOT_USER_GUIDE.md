# RIPPLE BD — Pilot User Guide

## What the participant does

Send the participant only the live link and this instruction:

> Open RIPPLE BD on your own phone or laptop. Complete all three scenarios by yourself. Choose what you would genuinely do first; do not try to guess the “correct” answer. When you finish and see your Digital Reflex Profile, return to the pilot form and answer the short post-experience questions. Please do not discuss your choices with other participants until everyone has finished.

## Facilitator rules

Do:
- Give each participant an anonymous code such as RBD-001.
- Record start and finish time.
- Let the participant navigate without coaching.
- Note where they hesitate, misread a label, or ask for help.
- Only help if they are genuinely stuck.
- Ask them to complete the post-experience questions immediately after the app.

Do not:
- Tell them which choice is safer.
- Explain REWIND, X-Ray, SHIELD, or the score before they experience it.
- Collect names, phone numbers, religion, ethnicity, political preference, victim history, or harassment history.
- Claim that the in-app score is a validated psychological measure.
- Share one participant's answers with another participant before completion.

## What counts as a completed pilot

A participant is complete when they:
1. finish the baseline mini-scenarios;
2. complete all three RIPPLE scenarios;
3. reach the Digital Reflex Profile;
4. complete the post-experience questions.

## What you need to send back for analysis

After the pilot, provide the filled CSV or Google Sheet export. The final analysis will calculate:
- N invited, started and completed;
- completion rate;
- baseline DPDS;
- post DPDS;
- DPDS change;
- median completion time;
- average usability ratings;
- top confusion point;
- one product change driven by pilot evidence;
- one anonymous quote only if quote consent was given.

This is a rapid preliminary convenience pilot, not a causal impact study.
