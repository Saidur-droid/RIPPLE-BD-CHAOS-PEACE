# RIPPLE BD — Rapid Pilot Form

Use this as the exact structure for a Google Form or paper-assisted pilot. Do not collect names, phone numbers, religion, ethnicity, political preference, victim history, or harassment history.

## Section 1 — Consent
1. Participant code (required; format RBD-001)
2. Age confirmation: I am 18 or older. [Yes / No]
3. I understand this is a fictional learning simulation and I can stop at any time. [Yes / No]
4. I consent to anonymous use of my responses for product evaluation. [Yes / No]

If any answer above is No, stop.

## Section 2 — Baseline mini-scenarios
Score each response 0–25 using the scoring key below.

### Verification
A short viral clip has no original source. What do you do first?
- Share before it disappears — 0
- Comment angrily — 5
- Wait and trace the original source/context — 25
- Ignore without checking — 12

### Manipulation recognition
Which signal most strongly suggests you should slow down?
- Many likes — 5
- Urgent language + missing source + cropped context — 25
- A friend shared it — 5
- The caption is confident — 0

### Bystander response
A classmate is being mocked in a group chat. What is the safest first response?
- Join the joke — 0
- Re-share privately — 0
- Check on the target and ask what support they want — 25
- Publicly attack the harassers — 10

### Support + de-escalation
A sweeping claim targets a fictional community without direct evidence. Best next step?
- Add another generalization — 0
- Share it with a warning — 8
- Separate claim from evidence and seek relevant primary context — 25
- Say nothing and assume it is true — 5

Baseline DPDS = sum / 100.

## Section 3 — Experience
Participant completes RIPPLE BD independently. Record:
- Start time
- End time
- Completed all 3 scenarios? [Yes / No]
- Needed facilitator help? [None / Minor / Major]
- Where did they hesitate? [short text]

## Section 4 — Parallel post mini-scenarios
Use different wording/context but the same four dimensions and the same 0–25 scoring logic. Do not repeat the exact baseline questions.

## Section 5 — Usability
Rate 1–5:
- I understood how my first choice changed the simulation.
- REWIND was easy to understand.
- The safer retry felt useful rather than preachy.
- Manipulation X-Ray helped me notice something I had missed.
- SHIELD Mode gave me a clear sequence for supporting someone.
- I would recommend this experience to another young person.

Open response:
- What is one thing you learned without looking back at the app?
- What was the most confusing screen?
- What should we improve first?
- Optional anonymous quote for the pitch deck. [Yes, you may quote this anonymously / No]

## Reporting
Report N invited, N started, N completed, completion rate, mean baseline DPDS, mean immediate post DPDS, median completion time, top confusion point, one product change, and missing data. Describe this as a preliminary convenience pilot, not causal validation.
