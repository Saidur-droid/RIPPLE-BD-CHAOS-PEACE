# RIPPLE BD — CHAOS → PEACE

> **Every click has a consequence.**

**RIPPLE BD** is a mobile-first digital peace simulator for Bangladeshi youth. Participants experience how ordinary online decisions can amplify misinformation, cyberbullying, gendered abuse, polarization, and exclusion; rewind the consequences; practise safer responses; and measure whether those digital peacebuilding reflexes last.

**Core experience:** CHAOS → REWIND → PEACE  
**Protection module:** SHIELD Mode  
**Campaign:** #EveryClickRipples  
**Primary DKC theme:** Responsible Digital Citizenship  
**Cross-cutting lens:** Digital Literacy  
**Target:** DKC Digital Respect & Cohesion Fellowship 2026

## Why this exists

Young people do not encounter misinformation, cyberbullying, religious tension, misogyny, and exclusion in separate classrooms. They encounter them in the same feed, often inside the same chain of clicks.

RIPPLE BD turns digital-safety education from passive information into:
1. decisions,
2. visible consequences,
3. rewind and retry,
4. safe-response practice,
5. measurable learning,
6. retention testing.

## Product thesis

> People learn digital peacebuilding more deeply when they can safely experience the consequences of a decision, reverse it, practise a better response, and see what changed.

## MVP

The first production-grade MVP is a **6–8 minute, mobile-first web experience** with:
- three fictional Bangladesh-relevant scenarios,
- baseline decision capture,
- the Ripple Engine,
- REWIND transition,
- SHIELD Mode,
- Manipulation X-Ray,
- personalized Digital Reflex Profile,
- Day-3 and Day-7 booster-ready flows,
- zero paid APIs or services.

## Operating principles

- **No fake data.** Pilot numbers are published only after collection.
- **No fake partnerships.** Referral/partner claims require confirmation.
- **No real victim content in the simulator.** Scenarios are fictionalized and safety-reviewed.
- **No paid dependency for the MVP.**
- **No unnecessary AI.** Deterministic interactions beat unreliable demos.
- **Privacy by default.** Collect only what is needed for evaluation.
- **One polished vertical slice beats twenty unfinished features.**
