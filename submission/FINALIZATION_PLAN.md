## Information to add later

### A. Team block
Provide:
- Full name of each team member
- Age / eligibility confirmation
- Division
- Role
- 1–2 line relevant background

Recommended 3-person role split:
1. Product & Technology
2. Research, M&E & Safeguarding
3. Community, Creative & Pitch

If there are 4–5 members, add Operations/Partnerships and Visual/Content roles.

### B. Pilot block
Provide actual pilot export with:
- participant codes only
- baseline scores
- post scores
- completion time
- usability answers
- confusion points
- feedback
- quote consent and anonymous quote where applicable

## Finalization sequence after those inputs arrive
1. Validate and clean pilot data.
2. Calculate DPDS and completion metrics.
3. Produce one baseline-vs-post chart and one concise evidence table.
4. Update product if pilot exposes a critical usability issue.
5. Replace all pilot placeholders in the 8-slide deck.
6. Add team details and complementary-role evidence.
7. Tighten initiative description to the official word limit.
8. Insert live QR and final screenshots into the pitch deck.
9. Run a claim audit: no fabricated evidence, no causal overclaim, no unverified partner claim.
10. Run a final submission checklist and freeze the build.

## Submission evidence language
Use:
“Preliminary convenience pilot with N participants.”

Do not use:
“Proven impact,” “scientifically validated,” or causal claims unless supported by an appropriate study design.
