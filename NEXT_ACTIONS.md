## P0 — evidence before cosmetics

1. **Lock the eligible team**
   - 3–5 members
   - all from the same division
   - each age 18–35
   - confirm bootcamp/showcase commitment
   - assign Product/Tech, Research/M&E/Safety, Community/Creative/Pitch ownership

2. **Run reliability QA**
   - 360px Android-like viewport

4. **Start the real pilot**
   - first 8–10 users immediately
   - target 20–30 eligible local youth
   - use the existing rapid pilot instrument
   - collect baseline, post, completion time, confusion points, and quote consent

   - median completion time
   - top confusion point
   - missing data

   - what users struggled with
   - what changed
   - why the change addresses that evidence

7. Produce one clean baseline-vs-post chart with the sample size and the line:
   - “Preliminary convenience pilot; uncontrolled; not a causal estimate.”

   - judge chooses a first action
   - consequence unfolds
   - human consequence appears

9. Prepare demo failure protection:
   - tested QR
   - direct URL
   - offline/recorded backup demo
   - screenshots of key states

10. Freeze submission:
   - max 8 slides

   - real pilot numbers only
   - verified team only

## Do not build before submission

Do not add AI chat, accounts, native apps, dashboards, social-media integrations, or a backend unless a concrete pilot failure proves they are necessary.

**The competitive moat now is: product reliability + local evidence + visible iteration + memorable REWIND demo.**
