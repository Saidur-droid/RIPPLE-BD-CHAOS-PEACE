## Current documented rules

### Team
- **3–5 members**
- members should be from the **same division**
- applicant age range: **18–35**
- women and marginalized applicants are encouraged by the program

### Application
- initiative description: **maximum 500 words**
- pitch deck: **maximum 8 slides**
- include a measurable outcome achievable by **February 2027**
- select a clear theme and show direct alignment

### Deadline
**30 September 2026, 11:59 PM Bangladesh time**

RIPPLE internal deadline:
**29 September 2026**

## RIPPLE theme choice

**Primary theme:** Responsible Digital Citizenship  
**Cross-cutting lens:** Digital Literacy

Other themes appear as scenario contexts, but the application should not claim to equally solve all five themes.

## Published selection logic we must design for

The DKC application guidance identifies factors including:
- relevance and potential impact,
- complementary team strength,
- implementation feasibility,
- local community connection,
- theme alignment,
- commitment to fellowship/bootcamp/showcase activities.

## Non-negotiable integrity rules

Even if competition pressure is high, do not:
- fake participant numbers,
- fake partnerships,
- invent endorsements,
- submit copied content as original,
- fabricate quotes,
- hide negative pilot results,
- use real victims without consent,
- imply a prototype feature exists if it does not.

The strongest application is a credible one.
